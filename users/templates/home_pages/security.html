Security at CyberShield

Enterprise‑grade protection for your most critical assets

Encryption Everywhere

All data is encrypted at rest with AES‑256 and in transit via TLS 1.3. Keys are managed with strict access controls.

Secure Infrastructure

Hosted on AWS with SOC 2 certified data centers. Network segmentation, firewalls, and DDoS protection safeguard every layer.

Compliance Ready

Our controls align with ISO 27001, SOC 2, and GDPR. We undergo regular third‑party audits.

Continuous Monitoring

We monitor our systems 24/7 for anomalies and maintain a public bug bounty program to catch vulnerabilities fast.

Report a Security Vulnerability

If you believe you've discovered a security issue in our platform, please report it through our Vulnerability Disclosure Program or email us directly.

Access Control & Authentication

We enforce strong password policies and offer SAML‑based Single Sign‑On (SSO) for enterprise plans. Multi‑factor authentication (MFA) is required for all administrative accounts.

Data Retention & Deletion

Customer data is retained only as long as necessary to provide the service. Upon termination, data is securely deleted within 30 days according to our data sanitization policy.
